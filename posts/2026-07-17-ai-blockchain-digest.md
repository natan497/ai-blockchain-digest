## Artificial Intelligence
### Patreon stops asking AI bots not to scrape — and starts blocking them
Patreon is taking a more proactive approach to defending against AI scraping by working with Cloudflare to block bots that train AI models on creators' content without permission. This move marks a significant shift away from relying solely on websites using robots.txt to prevent unauthorized AI training. By actively blocking these bots, Patreon aims to better protect its creators' content and intellectual property. This development is crucial for developers, as it highlights the importance of robust security measures in preventing AI-related copyright infringement.

### Why the first GPU financiers are turning to inference chips in a $400 million deal
A recent $400 million chip-backed loan has brought attention to the growing trend of AI infrastructure deals, particularly in the realm of inference chips. This shift is significant, as it indicates that investors are recognizing the potential of specialized chips in supporting the development of more efficient AI systems. For developers, this trend is worth noting, as it may lead to the creation of more targeted and effective AI solutions. The increased focus on inference chips could also drive innovation in areas such as edge computing and real-time data processing.

### The AI compute gap: Enterprises are buying infrastructure faster than they can measure what it costs
Enterprises are rapidly investing in AI infrastructure, but many are struggling to accurately measure the costs associated with these investments. A recent study found that most organizations are running their AI on familiar hyperscalers and model-provider APIs, yet they are planning to switch or add providers in the near future. This shift is driven by factors such as integration and total cost of ownership, rather than just the headline token price. Developers should be aware of this trend, as it highlights the need for more transparent and efficient AI infrastructure solutions. The inability to track unit economics and the underutilization of GPUs are also key challenges that developers should address in their own projects.

### The agent security gap: 54% of enterprises have already had an AI agent incident, and most still let agents share credentials
A significant security gap has been identified in the way enterprises are handling AI agents, with over half of organizations having experienced a confirmed agent security incident or near-miss. The main issue is that AI agents are being given real access to systems and data without adequate controls in place. Most agents still share credentials, and only a few organizations are taking steps to isolate high-risk agents. Developers should prioritize building more secure AI agent systems, focusing on purpose-built security stacks and scoped identities for each agent. This will help mitigate the risks associated with AI-enabled attacks and protect sensitive data.

### Google Vids now lets you star in your own AI videos
Google has introduced a new feature in its Vids platform, allowing users to create personalized AI videos starring a digital version of themselves. This innovation is made possible by Gemini Omni-powered tools, which enable users to generate and edit videos from prompts and reference images. For developers, this development is notable, as it demonstrates the growing potential of AI in creative applications. The ability to create customized AI avatars and videos could have significant implications for industries such as entertainment, education, and marketing.

For developers, this development is notable, as it indicates the growing interest of major financial institutions in the cryptocurrency space.
