## Blockchain

### Ethereum Glamsterdam Upgrade Targets L2 Settlement Costs

Ethereum's **Glamsterdam** upgrade introduces Enshrined Proposer-Builder Separation (ePBS) and optimized gas mechanics specifically designed to reduce settlement costs for Layer-2 networks. For teams building on Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, or zkSync, cheaper L1 settlement means lower rollup fees and better UX at the application layer. No hard-fork date is set yet, but testnets are expected within weeks.

### DTCC Moves Toward On-Chain Asset Tokenization

Following a December 2025 SEC no-action letter, the DTCC's DTC arm is planning **limited live tokenization transactions in July 2026** and a broader launch in October. This is arguably the most significant institutional DeFi move in years — the DTCC clears roughly $2 quadrillion in securities annually. Developers building compliant tokenization platforms should track the DTC's technical specification releases closely.

### NEAR Protocol's Dynamic Resharding Goes Live

NEAR's **dynamic resharding** upgrade is scheduled to activate in June 2026, allowing the network to automatically provision additional shards as transaction volume grows. For developers on NEAR, this means fewer capacity bottlenecks during traffic spikes.
